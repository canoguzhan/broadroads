OFFICIAL GUIDE TO BROADROADS CHAMPIONS, ALCHEMY & COMBAT

🛡️ Champion Archetypes & Playstyles

Every champion in Broadroads brings specialized tactical strengths to the arena floor:

  • ⚔️ Melee Vanguard (Paladin / Bruiser): Frontline juggernauts equipped with heavy shields and sweeping blades. High base armor, defensive barrier regeneration, and crowd-clearing radial slashes. Ideal for holding the central Anvil.
  • 🏹 Ranged Marksman (Gunner / Ranger): High mobility skirmishers armed with twin blasters or celestial bows. Excellent at kiting foes from afar, executing priority threats, and dodging danger zones.
  • 🔮 Arcane Arcanist (Mage / Seer): High burst spellcasters commanding elemental singularities and gravitational rifts. Unmatched area-of-effect wave destruction and enemy grouping.

⚡ The Celestial Overcharge (Ultimate)

When the combat energy threshold is reached (Key: R), your champion channels the celestial ley lines for 10 seconds: granting +60% Attack Damage, +40% Movement Speed, an instant radial shockwave, and a radiant golden combat aura.

💎 The Four Elemental Minerals

Slain enemies release crystallized elemental energy across the arena:

  • 🔥 Pyrite Ore: Concentrated fire essence. Empowers blade sharpness, physical attack damage, and critical strikes.
  • 💧 Aether Shard: Flowing liquid crystal. Bolsters energetic shields, spell haste, and dash cooldown recovery.
  • 🛡️ Titanium Crystal: Hardened earthen geomatter. Grants maximum health pools, damage reduction plating, and tenacity.
  • ✨ Catalyst Core: Rare celestial relic ore. Multiplies ultimate power and unlocks legendary passive enchantments.

🎯 Navigation & Controls

Broadroads features an intuitive RTS hybrid control scheme:

  • Right-Click Ground: Issues a precision League-of-Legends target movement command with an animated green ripple.
  • Right-Click Enemy: Automatically approaches and locks onto the selected horror to strike.
  • WASD / Touch Joystick: Direct manual directional movement for dodging skillshots.
  • Q / Space / Left Click: Primary weapon strike.
  • W / Shift / D: Tactical dash with invulnerability frames.
  • E: Unleashes a radial shockwave nova pushing enemies back.
  • R: Triggers the Celestial Overcharge ultimate.
  • F: Toggles Auto-Attack target locking.
  • B: Channels return to the Refinery forge.

👥 Squad Survival & Revivals

In online co-op, downed squadmates enter a revival state. Surviving allies can stand inside their revival ring for 3 seconds to restore them directly into combat!

👾 Void Horrors & Minion Threats

Horrors emerge from outer portals seeking to extinguish the central Anvil:

  • Void Crawlers: Swarming fast melee minions that close distance quickly. Easy to cleave down with Novas.
  • Corrupted Stalkers: Armored beasts with lunging attacks. Requires dodging before landing heavy strikes.
  • Spire Artillery: Distant horrors that launch explosive mortar projectiles across the arena floor.

💀 Ignis Rex - Anvil Overlord

The colossal wave 5 Overlord towers above the arena with three deadly combat phases:

  • Phase 1 (Anvil Slam): Ignis slams the earth, producing expanding magma shockwaves. Jump or dash over the wave.
  • Phase 2 (Molten Swarm): Portals erupt around Ignis summoning elite escort horrors. Focus down the escorts while maintaining distance.
  • Phase 3 (Enraged Core): Below 30% health, Ignis radiates continuous heat damage. Pop Celestial Overcharge (R) and focus all damage to claim victory!
